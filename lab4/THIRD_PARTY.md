# Lab 4 data and model provenance

The five measured episode identifiers are preserved in `replay_assets/provenance.json`.
These are user-provided recordings of Diana7 + Paxini DexH13, with module ID3 SDK
force output and recorded motion. Compact NPZ files retain the original selected
timestamps, joint states, measured forces and source indices. They are not new
measurements and do not contain hardware-control code.

The platform MJCF originates from [kiramint/Dexman_Lerobot](https://github.com/kiramint/Dexman_Lerobot),
branch `dexhand_teleop`, commit `da0f0904d1b5d237b1c0eccbdeb7862a7fb14beb`,
`models/mujoco_dexh13/`. The prior replay project froze inactive joints, added
tactile sites, mapped joint names, and configured collision filtering and control.
Arm mounting transforms use the SDK main snapshot
`e8db52f4e3a335236c7c2ce65c16aa29065fe6ba` from
[kiramint/Dexman_ROS2_SDK](https://github.com/kiramint/Dexman_ROS2_SDK).

The experiment fixture originated from a user-provided scene archive. Taxel sites
originate from user-provided tactile templates; their normals are geometry-based
estimates, not independently certified manufacturer metrology. The stored source
replays include their historical +Y 0.5 mm fixture setting. New synthetic shifts
are additional, per-sequence values recorded in `datasets/manifest.json`.

`import_source.py` rewrites only MJCF asset paths, stores each unique mesh once,
and compares compact and original geometry numerically. Mesh content hashes and
original asset names are in `replay_assets/provenance.json`; original replay/model
hashes are retained with each episode. Joint angles and measured labels are not
changed. The compact snapshots are for saved-state kinematic playback; they do
not include the original full-rate, 1140-taxel HDF5 files.

Synthetic signals are generated by the educational model in `prepare.py`. They
are explicitly labelled `synthetic_sensor`, not hardware measurements or a
validated digital twin. No new ownership or licence over third-party model
assets is asserted; the original owners' terms continue to apply.
